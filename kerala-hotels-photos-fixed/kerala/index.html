A STATE OF MANY STORIES

Small in size.
Rich in stories.

Kerala is a meeting of landscapes and cultures — a narrow strip between the Arabian Sea and the Western Ghats. Every district has its own character: a different food tradition, a festival, a craft, a coastline or a mountain path.

This guide brings those stories together, district by district, so you can explore beyond the familiar postcard.
