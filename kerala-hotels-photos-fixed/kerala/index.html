Optimal Sojourn Timing

Seasons of God's Own Country.
When to Travel.

Kerala's climate transforms through three magnificent cycles. Choose the mood that aligns with your wanderlust.

PEAK EXPLORATION

Winter Radiance
October to March

22°C – 30°C · Mild & Golden

The premier window for tranquil backwater cruises, coastal beach retreats in Kovalam and Marari, and cool mountain rambles across Munnar and Wayanad. Minimal rain, gentle humidity, and vibrant temple festivals.

Best For: Sightseeing, Houseboats, Beach Relaxation
AYURVEDA & EMERALD RAIN

Monsoon Symphony
June to September

20°C – 28°C · Rain-Swept & Lush

Kerala in its poetic majesty. Roaring waterfalls like Athirappilly and Perunthenaruvi, misty plantation ridges, and the traditional season for authentic Panchakarma Ayurvedic rejuvenation as pores open naturally in the humid cool.

Best For: Ayurveda Rejuvenation, Waterfalls, Nature Photography
HIGHLAND RETREATS

Summer Serenity
April to May

26°C – 35°C · Warm Coastal Days

While the coastal plains warm up, the high-altitude cloud forests of Idukki, Vagamon, and Wayanad offer crisp cool mountain escapes. Famous for dramatic temple poorams featuring majestic percussion orchestras (Chenda Melam).

Best For: Munnar Tea Hills, Thrissur Pooram Festival, Wildlife Safaris
